# About StackVerdict

StackVerdict publishes reviews, comparisons and buying guides for AI marketing software: SEO, content-writing and ad creative tools. We write for bloggers, freelance writers and small marketing teams who want to know which tool is worth paying for before they hand over a card.

## How we review

Each review is built from the vendor's own documentation and pricing pages, verified user reviews on sites like G2, Capterra and Trustpilot, and hands-on use where we have it. We say which is which. When a tool has a real weakness, we name it, including tools we earn a commission from.

Pricing and features change often. Every article shows the date it was last checked; always confirm the current price on the vendor's site before buying.
