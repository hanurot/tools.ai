# Privacy Policy

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not sell personal data. Affiliate partners may set cookies when you click their links, so they can credit a referral; those cookies are governed by the partner's own privacy policy. If we add analytics, this page will say which tool we use and what it collects.

## Email newsletter

If you enter your email address in our signup form, we use it only to send you the free Frase Playbook and occasional new reviews and tutorials. Your address is stored by our email provider, MailerLite, which processes it on our behalf. We never sell or share it. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time by replying to any of our emails.
