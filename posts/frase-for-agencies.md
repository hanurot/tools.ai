## How Frase is organized around client sites

Frase is built around a **site**, which maps well to an agency's client list. When you add a site, Frase reads the root domain and builds a **Brand Hub**: positioning, audience personas, brand values, matching criteria and tracked competitors. In our test, onboarding took about 2–3 minutes and found three competitors automatically. That profile then feeds every brief and draft for that site.

According to Frase's agency page, each client gets a separate workspace with its own research, briefs, optimization history and citation tracking. It also lists team roles (owner, admin, editor, viewer), which matters when freelancers or junior writers join a project.

For agency work, the tools that matter most per client are:

- **Site audit.** Frase discovered 500+ URLs on our demo site and audited the first 20 pages automatically, flagging issues such as pages AI can't read, missing meta descriptions and broken internal links. Audit pages are a monthly allowance, so point them at money pages first.
- **AI Visibility.** Tracks prompts across AI engines and shows visibility %, share of voice, average position and which domains AI answers cite. In our trial, 93% of cited sources for the demo prompt were editorial sites and none were the brand's own, which is a ready-made talking point for a client meeting.
- **Competitive Analysis.** Paste a client page and up to three competitor URLs (or compare against top results) to see what the outranking pages cover that yours doesn't.
- **Automations.** Playbooks for comparison articles, listicles, SEO blog posts and quick updates, plus rules like "Publish-Ready" that can stop a publish. In our trial every playbook paused for human approval.

Starter is a solo plan (one seat, one domain), so it doesn't really work for an agency. The useful comparison is Professional vs Scale.

**Per-client cost if every domain slot is filled (yearly billing):**

- **Professional:** $103 ÷ 5 clients ≈ **$21 per client per month**, with 8 articles per client.
- **Scale:** $239 ÷ 10 clients ≈ **$24 per client per month**, with 10 articles per client and two more AI engines.

Both are small next to a typical content retainer. The real limit is volume. If one client needs 20 articles a month, it uses half of Professional's allowance by itself.

Two credit rules to know before you plan volume:

- **"Improve scores" and "Rewrite" each use one article credit.** Agencies that let writers click these freely will burn through the allowance quickly. Fix E-E-A-T issues by hand instead.
- **Domains, seats and AI-visibility prompts are concurrent limits** in our trial, meaning they don't reset monthly. Plan domain slots around your steady client list, not one-off projects.

## The white-label catch

Frase's agency page lists **white-label reports, branded client portals and custom domains as Enterprise-only**. Scale adds multi-site, multi-seat and exportable reports, and Frase says audits, briefs and research can be exported in forwardable formats on lower plans.

What that means in practice:

- If your clients are happy receiving a brief or an audit export and a monthly summary you write yourself, Professional or Scale is fine.
- If you resell SEO under your own brand and clients must never see a vendor's name, you'll need an Enterprise quote or a separate reporting tool.

Frase's settings menu also showed a **Client Portal** entry during our trial, but we didn't test it and its availability on each plan isn't something we could confirm.

**1. Prospect audits and AI visibility give you a sales story.** Frase says it can score a prospect's site for AI visibility in under a minute. Showing a prospect that ChatGPT or Google AI recommends their rivals, and cites sites that don't include theirs, is a stronger pitch than a generic SEO report.

**2. Briefs that junior writers can follow.** Our brief came with a core argument, "what the reader wants", sourced key facts with ✓ Verified badges, content gaps and a section-by-section outline with word budgets. That's the structure an editor usually writes by hand. See our [best content brief generators](/best-content-brief-tools/) for how it compares.

**3. GEO scoring is a sellable add-on.** Our draft scored **94% GEO** and **78% SEO**. Agencies can package "optimized for AI answers" as part of the retainer. If clients ask what that means, point them to [what GEO is](/what-is-geo/).

**4. Flat pricing with every feature on every plan.** The AI agent, SEO and GEO optimization, AI-visibility tracking, Content Guard, site audits, API/MCP access and Brand Voice are included from Starter up. Higher plans raise volume, not feature access, apart from the Enterprise-only items above.

## Where it falls short

- **E-E-A-T is weak in AI drafts.** Our draft scored 33%: Experience 5% and Authority 0%. Client expertise, original images and author credentials have to be added by people.
- **Drafts need a structure check.** While finishing, our draft listed some headings twice. It had zero images and three links.
- **Root domains only.** A subpath site (like `name.github.io/blog`) failed to load during onboarding. Some client setups may need a workaround.
- **Reviewer complaints.** On [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/197955/Frase/reviews/){: rel="nofollow" } and G2, ratings are high overall, but some reviewers mention occasional bugs, an AI writer that doesn't always follow instructions, and limited non-English support.

## Who should buy, and who shouldn't

**Frase is a good fit if your agency:**

- manages SEO content for 3–10 client sites
- writes the articles in-house or with a small freelancer pool
- wants to add AI-search visibility reporting to retainers
- prefers predictable flat pricing over per-user billing

**Look elsewhere if your agency:**

- needs fully white-labelled client reports today and won't go Enterprise
- has 20+ writers who only need a grader
- works mainly in languages other than English
- runs technical SEO work that needs a dedicated crawler or rank tracker (Frase's audit is useful but not a replacement)

## FAQ

**Which Frase plan is best for a small agency?**
Professional for up to 5 client domains and 40 articles a month. Scale if you have up to 10 clients, need 100 articles or want AI tracking on Claude and Gemini too.

**Does Frase offer white-label reports?**
Only on Enterprise, according to Frase's agency page. Lower plans can export audits, briefs and research, but with standard branding.

**Can I add more team members?**
Yes. Extra seats cost $29/month each on Professional and Scale. Starter has one seat and doesn't offer extras.

**Is there a free trial for agencies?**
Yes, 7 days. Frase's pricing page says no credit card is required. Our trial allowed 5 articles, 15 research runs, 50 audit pages and 3 team seats.

**Can Frase publish directly to client sites?**
Frase lists WordPress, Webflow, Sanity, Wix and FraseCMS as publishing targets. Auto-publishing is optional and off by default on the plans that offer it.
