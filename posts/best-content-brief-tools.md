## How we judged them

We did not run hands-on tests of the other six tools for this article. Their facts come from vendor pages and independent pricing trackers, and we say so where it matters.

A brief tool earns its price on five things:

1. **Reader intent:** does it say what the searcher wants and what they'll do next?
2. **Gap finding:** does it show what the top results *miss*, not just what they share?
3. **Evidence:** does it hand the writer citable facts with sources?
4. **Outline quality:** does each section have a purpose, a word budget and talking points?
5. **Cost per brief** at your real monthly volume.

## 2. Content Harmony: best brief-only tool for teams

Content Harmony is built around one job: producing standardized briefs that you hand to writers. Its own positioning is that teams build detailed content marketing briefs 50-80% faster than manual briefs.

- **Pricing is per "workflow"** (a keyword report, brief and grading for one piece). Plans start at $50 per month for 5 Content Workflows, with unlimited users, unlimited projects and rollover credits, which works out to an average of about $8 per workflow.
- **Low-risk trial:** the introductory offer gives you your first 10 content workflows for $10.
- **Strengths:** one reviewer called its briefs the strongest in this category. Unlimited seats make it easy to assign briefs to many writers.
- **Weaknesses:** it doesn't write the draft for you, and the same reviewer notes it lacks the real-time editing experience of Surfer SEO.

**Pick it if** you run a freelance-writer team and want consistent briefs without paying per seat.

## 3. MarketMuse: best for topic strategy

MarketMuse thinks at the level of **topic clusters**: which pages you need to build authority on a subject, not just one brief.

- **Free plan:** it has a free tier with 10 content queries per month, enough to see whether its topic model suits you.
- **Paid plans:** these have moved to quotes. As of November 2026, one tracker lists it with a free plan; paid plans are priced by quote, while mid-2026 listings showed Optimize at $99/month, Research at $249/month, and Strategy at $499/month.

**Pick it if** you plan content for a whole site or client portfolio and have a strategy budget. For a solo blogger it's more tool than needed.

## How to get more from any brief tool

1. **Fix the reader first.** A brief built for the wrong audience produces the wrong article. In Frase, check the brand profile before your first brief.
2. **Pin the gaps, not just the keywords.** The "what competitors miss" section is what lets a new site outrank older ones.
3. **Approve facts deliberately.** Reject anything unverified before writing.
4. **Edit the brief, not the draft.** Changing a brief is cheap; rewriting an article costs time (and, in Frase, a credit).
5. **Add what AI can't:** your own tests, photos and opinions. That's what lifts EEAT.

## FAQ

**What is a content brief generator?**
A tool that analyzes the top search results for a keyword and turns them into instructions for a writer: audience, angle, outline, questions to answer, terms to cover and facts to cite.

**Is a brief tool worth it if I write my own articles?**
Yes, if you publish regularly. A good brief stops you from missing subtopics the top results cover and shows you the angle they miss. If you also want the draft, choose a tool that writes from its brief (Frase, Clearscope, MarketMuse).

**Which tool has the cheapest briefs?**
Per brief, Content Harmony's trial (10 briefs for $10) is the cheapest way to start. For ongoing use with drafting included, Frase Starter is the best value in our comparison.

**Can I use these briefs with ChatGPT or Claude?**
Yes. Export or copy the brief and use it as the prompt. Frase also connects to AI assistants through its MCP integration.
