## Why AI visibility matters now

Traditional SEO tells you where a page ranks on Google. It doesn't tell you whether an AI answer recommends you, and those answers increasingly *are* the shortlist. Optimizing for them is called GEO (Generative Engine Optimization); our [guide to GEO](/what-is-geo/) covers the content side. AI visibility tracking is the **measurement** side: without it you're guessing.

## Step 1: Open AI Visibility

In Frase, go to **Monitor → AI Visibility**. During onboarding Frase reads your site, builds a brand profile and suggests competitors to track, so the dashboard starts with you plus up to three rivals.

The header shows prompts used against your limit (ours read **2/5** on the trial) and an **Add prompt** button. Tabs: Overview, Trends, Prompts, AI referrals, Recommendations and Logs.

## Step 2: Read the four headline numbers

| Metric | What it means | What we saw (demo brand) |
|---|---|---|
| **Visibility** | Share of AI answers that mention you | 50% (1 of 2 answers) |
| **Share of voice** | Your mentions vs. tracked competitors' mentions | 100% |
| **Average position** | Where you appear in the answers that mention you | #1.0 |
| **Sentiment** | Whether the answers speak well of you | Positive (1 answer read) |

Competitors appear in the same table for visibility, share of voice and position. **Competitors aren't read for sentiment**, only you.

**Don't over-read small samples.** With two prompts, one answer moves visibility by 50 points. Track at least the full prompt allowance before drawing conclusions.

## Step 3: Check the buyer journey

Frase groups prompts into **Awareness, Consideration and Decision**. In our test, the brand appeared in every Consideration answer but **missed the only Awareness prompt** ("What are the best sustainable sneakers for everyday wear and travel in 2026?"). That's a classic gap: a brand known to people already comparing it, invisible to people just starting to look.

**Action:** write top-of-funnel content (best-of lists, explainers) for the Awareness questions you miss.

## Step 4: Use citation sources (the most valuable part)

Frase lists the domains that AI answers cite, split into **Editorial** (blogs, guides, news) and **Community** (Reddit, Quora, YouTube). In our test, **13 sites** were cited, 93% editorial and 7% community, and **none of them was the brand's own site**.

That tells you something specific: for that question, the AI trusts third-party round-ups more than the brand's own pages. To get mentioned, you need to be **in the articles the AI is already reading**.

## Step 5: Work through Recommendations

The **Recommendations** tab turns gaps into a to-do list, each with a goal, a result and a priority:

![Frase AI Visibility recommendations: get cited and close gaps](/img/frase/10_ai_recommendations.jpg)

- **Get cited:** "Get into the articles AI quotes". Our test showed **3 sites to pitch** (medium priority).
- **Close gaps:** "Be the first answer to 1 open question". This was the missed Awareness prompt (low priority).

## How to choose good prompts

Your prompt limit is small and **concurrent** (it doesn't reset monthly), so choose prompts carefully:

1. **Use real buyer phrasing.** Write "best AI SEO tool for freelance writers", not "AI SEO software".
2. **Cover all three stages.** Include one Awareness prompt ("best…"), one Consideration prompt ("X vs Y"), and one Decision prompt ("is X worth it").
3. **Include your money keyword.** Use the question that leads to a purchase in your niche.
4. **Add a competitor-comparison prompt.** It shows who the AI names instead of you.
5. **Keep them stable.** Changing prompts every week breaks your trend line.

## A playbook for bloggers and affiliates

You don't need to be a brand for this to be useful. If you run a review site, track the prompts your readers ask, for example "best content brief tool" or "Frase vs Surfer". Then:

- **See which sites AI cites** for those questions. Those are the pages competing with yours, and their structure shows what AI likes to quote.
- **Write the article the AI is missing.** Use clear verdicts, comparison tables, standalone stats with named sources, and an FAQ. Our draft built this way scored 94% on Frase's GEO score.
- **Recheck monthly** to see whether your site starts appearing in the citation list.
