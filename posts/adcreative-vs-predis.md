## The core difference

- **AdCreative.ai** is an **ad-performance tool**. Generating is unlimited; you pay a credit only when you download a creative. On top of generation it adds a 0–100 creative score and insights from your connected ad accounts.
- **Predis.ai** is an **AI content studio plus scheduler**. It generates social posts, carousels, ad creatives and videos from a prompt, product link or image, then publishes them to your channels. It also includes competitor analysis.

**Predis.ai** uses a credit pool shared across posts, ads and videos:

**Read the trial terms first.** One October 2026 write-up says every Predis plan now starts with a 3-day trial and the card is charged when it ends. Older pages that mention a permanent free plan appear to be out of date.

**What this means:** on raw cost per creative, Predis.ai is roughly 5× cheaper. AdCreative.ai only wins on value if its scoring and ad-account insights help you avoid spending money on weak ads. That matters most when your monthly ad budget is in the hundreds of dollars or more.

## Where AdCreative.ai is stronger

1. **Pre-launch scoring.** Every creative gets a predicted-performance score, so you test the most promising variations first.
2. **Learns from your ads.** Creative Insights reads connected ad accounts (CTR against your category, best colors and so on).
3. **Unlimited generation.** You can iterate as much as you like and only pay for what you download.
4. **Direct-response focus.** Layouts and copy are built for clicks and sales across ad sizes.

## Where Predis.ai is stronger

1. **Price per creative.** Much cheaper, especially billed yearly.
2. **Social + ads in one place.** Carousels, reels-style videos, captions, hashtags and a content calendar with publishing.
3. **Video on the entry plan.** AdCreative.ai needs the $249 plan for video.
4. **Competitor analysis** of social accounts, rare at this price.

## Weaknesses to know

**AdCreative.ai**

- Expensive per ad on Starter; 20 downloads go quickly.
- The score is a prediction, not proof. Only real spend tells you what works.
- There are user complaints about billing and cancellation. Cancel before the trial ends if it isn't for you, and keep the confirmation (see our [review](/adcreative-review/)).

**Predis.ai**

- Reviewers call the generator fairly basic unless you give it detailed prompts.
- Its scheduler lacks some features of dedicated social tools.
- A credit pool shared by images and video can run out faster than the headline numbers suggest.
- Plan names and prices have changed several times in 2026, which makes comparisons confusing.

## FAQ

**Is Predis.ai cheaper than AdCreative.ai?**
Yes, by a lot per creative: roughly $0.37 per ad image on Predis Core billed monthly, against about $1.95 on AdCreative.ai Starter. AdCreative.ai charges for its scoring and ad-account insights.

**Which one makes video ads?**
Both. Predis.ai includes video from its entry plan (using more credits); AdCreative.ai includes video from the $249/month Professional plan.

**Can Predis.ai post to social media automatically?**
Yes, it has a content calendar and publishing, with limits that vary by plan. AdCreative.ai doesn't schedule organic posts.

**Do they have free plans?**
AdCreative.ai has a 7-day trial. Predis.ai's current pricing page shows trials rather than a free plan, and recent reports say the card is charged when the trial ends, so check before you start.

**Can I use both?**
Yes. Some teams use Predis.ai for daily social content and AdCreative.ai for scored paid-ad variations.
