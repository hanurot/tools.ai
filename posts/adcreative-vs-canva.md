## The real difference in one sentence

**Canva helps you design an ad. AdCreative.ai generates dozens of ads for you to choose from.**

In Canva you pick a template and move elements yourself, with Magic Studio AI to speed up parts of the job. In AdCreative.ai you set up a brand (logo, colors, description) once, pick a size and goal, and it produces many finished variations at once.

## Where AdCreative.ai wins

**1. Volume of variations.** It produces many finished ads per click in the right sizes for each network. Testing 10 headlines × 3 images is a few minutes' work, not an afternoon.

**2. Creative scoring.** Each creative gets a 0–100 predicted-performance score, which helps you decide which variations to test first. Canva has nothing like this: you only learn what works after the ad runs.

**3. Ad-account insights.** Connect your ad accounts and it analyzes past ads (CTR against your category, best-performing colors, and so on).

**4. Built for direct response.** Layouts, copy frameworks and calls to action are designed for clicks and sales, not brand storytelling.

## Where Canva wins

**1. Design control.** You can move every element, choose fonts and fine-tune details. AdCreative.ai's output is faster but more template-like.

**2. One tool for everything.** Ads, Instagram posts, YouTube thumbnails, presentations, flyers, video edits. AdCreative.ai only does ads, ad copy and product shots.

**3. Price and the free plan.** A permanent free plan and about $18/month for Pro, with unlimited exports.

**4. Lower risk.** AdCreative.ai has a pattern of user complaints about billing and cancellation, covered in our [review](/adcreative-review/). Canva's billing is simple.

## Weaknesses to know

**AdCreative.ai**

- Starter's 20 downloads go quickly if you run several campaigns.
- Video ads need the $249/month Professional plan.
- The score is a prediction, not a guarantee. Only spend proves it.
- Cancel before the trial ends if it isn't for you, and keep the confirmation email.

**Canva**

- No performance prediction or ad-account insights.
- Making many variations still means editing each one, even with Magic Resize.
- Heavy AI image generation can use up the monthly Magic Studio credits.

## FAQ

**Is AdCreative.ai better than Canva for ads?**
For producing many test variations with a performance score, yes. For designing a few polished ads by hand, Canva is better and cheaper.

**Can Canva make ads with AI?**
Yes. Magic Studio can generate images, write copy and resize designs. But you still assemble and adjust each ad yourself, and there's no creative scoring.

**Does AdCreative.ai have a free plan?**
No. It has a 7-day trial. Canva has a permanent free plan.

**What does one ad cost on AdCreative.ai?**
About $1.95 on Starter ($39 for 20 downloads), dropping to about $0.83 on Professional. Generating and previewing are free; only downloads use credits.

**Is Canva Pro still about $18 a month?**
Most sources list Pro at $18/month or $144/year in 2026, though Canva has changed prices before and some regions differ. Check canva.com/pricing before buying.
