## 1. Frase — best overall

Frase starts from research rather than a prompt. It pulls keywords, the pages that rank, People Also Ask questions, content gaps and citable statistics, lets you pin what matters, then generates a brief and a full draft section by section. The editor scores the draft on SEO, GEO and EEAT separately.

![Frase research results with keywords, ranking pages, content gaps and evidence](/img/frase/13_research_results.jpg)

**Why it wins:** every plan includes every feature, including AI Visibility (ChatGPT and Google AI on Starter) and site audits. In our test, research took about 5 minutes and the ~1,500-word draft about 3–4 minutes.

**Watch out for:** EEAT is weak out of the box (33% for us), Starter is tight at 10 articles and 1 seat, and "Improve scores" or "Rewrite" each use an article credit.

**Price:** Starter $39/mo yearly or $49 monthly (10 articles), Professional $103/$129 (40), Scale $239/$299 (100). Full breakdown in our [Frase pricing guide](/frase-pricing/).

## 2. NeuronWriter — best budget pick

NeuronWriter is a content editor with NLP term recommendations, a SERP competitor view and AI writing templates. Plans run from Bronze at $23/month (25 documents, 2 projects) to Diamond at $117/month; yearly billing gives two months free. Gold and above add WordPress, Search Console, API and MCP access.

**Users like** the value compared with Surfer and the clear content score. **Users complain** that AI drafts need heavy editing, AI credits run out, and the interface feels dated. A 30-day refund applies to your first plan. See [Frase vs NeuronWriter](/frase-vs-neuronwriter/).

## 3. Surfer SEO — best on-page optimization editor

Surfer's Content Editor and Content Score are an industry standard, with Google Docs and WordPress integrations so writers optimize where they already work. Discovery is $49/month billed yearly; AI Tracker starts on Standard ($99/month yearly), and Pro ($182) tracks five AI engines daily, including Google AI Mode.

**Best for** teams that already have writers. **Less ideal** if you want the tool to research and draft from scratch. Read our [Frase vs Surfer SEO comparison](/frase-vs-surfer-seo/).

## 4. Clearscope — best premium content grade

Clearscope grades drafts from F to A++ against ranking pages and is loved for its simple interface. Essentials is $129/month (20 Drafts, 50 tracked pages and queries, unlimited users); Business is $399/month. Since its 2025 "2.0" relaunch it also tracks brand mentions in ChatGPT and Gemini. There's a 14-day free trial.

**Downside:** price, and fewer AI-writing features than Frase. G2 reviewers rate it 4.9/5 from about 90 reviews. See our [Frase vs Clearscope comparison](/frase-vs-clearscope/).

## 5. Semrush Content Toolkit — best if you already live in Semrush

Semrush's Content Toolkit costs $60/month (billed annually per its pricing page) and bundles Topic Finder, an SEO brief generator, a content optimizer, AI rewriting and one-click WordPress publishing. It has a 7-day trial. AI-search visibility is a separate Semrush product, not part of this toolkit.

## 6. Jasper — best for brand marketing copy

Jasper focuses on on-brand marketing content with Brand Voices, knowledge assets and AI agents. Pro is $69/seat monthly or $59 yearly; GEO and deep-research agents are Business-plan only (custom pricing). It's strong for campaign copy, but SEO research and scoring are lighter than dedicated tools. G2: 4.7/5 from over 1,300 reviews.

## 7. Scalenut — budget GEO article writer

[Scalenut](https://www.scalenut.com/){: rel="nofollow" } writes "GEO articles" and tracks prompts in ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews. List prices are $59, $89 and $199/month, but it runs frequent promotions that cut those sharply on annual plans. **Caution:** no refunds after the 7-day trial, and promotional deals are non-refundable.

## 8. Writesonic — best for AI visibility tracking first

[Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/){: rel="nofollow" } has pivoted toward AI search visibility: Starter ($79/month billed yearly) tracks 50 prompts in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews and includes 15 AI articles. Content optimization and fact-check features are Enterprise-only, and there are no refunds on cancellation.

## How to choose

- **You write from scratch and publish 8+ articles a month:** Frase.
- **You have writers and want to polish drafts:** Surfer or Clearscope.
- **You're on a tight budget:** NeuronWriter.
- **You already pay for Semrush:** Content Toolkit.
- **You care mostly about AI-search mentions:** Frase (cheapest entry) or Writesonic.

## Common weaknesses across all AI SEO writers

Every tool here produces drafts that need human editing. AI can't add your first-hand experience, photos, test results or credentials, which is what EEAT rewards. Budget time for fact-checking prices and product claims, adding original screenshots, and writing the verdict yourself.

## FAQ

**What is the best AI tool for SEO writing in 2026?**
For an all-in-one workflow at a small-business price, Frase. For pure optimization of human drafts, Surfer or Clearscope.

**What is the cheapest AI SEO writing tool?**
Among the tools here, NeuronWriter starts lowest at $23/month ($19/month billed yearly).

**Do AI SEO tools guarantee rankings?**
No. They help you match search intent and structure; rankings still depend on quality, links and experience signals.

**Which tools track ChatGPT and Google AI mentions?**
Frase (all plans), Surfer (Standard and up), Clearscope, Scalenut, Writesonic and NeuronWriter (as an add-on).

**Can I use AI-written content without a penalty?**
Google says it rewards helpful content regardless of how it's made. Unedited, thin AI content is the risk.
