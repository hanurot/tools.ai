## Price: Frase is roughly a third of the cost

For a solo writer, Clearscope Essentials at $129/month costs about 2.6× Frase Starter monthly ($49) and 3.3× billed yearly ($39). Clearscope's annual pricing isn't published; it's arranged through sales.

Clearscope's value improves for teams: every plan includes **unlimited users**, while Frase charges $29/month per extra seat on Professional and Scale. A 10-person content team may find Clearscope Business simpler to budget.

## Writing: Frase drafts, Clearscope grades

**Frase** is built to produce a first draft. Research takes about 5 minutes, the brief under a minute, and Frase writes the article section by section while the score updates.

![Frase writing an article section by section](/img/frase/19_writing_live.jpg)

**Clearscope** is built around grading. Writers paste or write in its editor (or connect their workflow) and aim for an A or A++ grade as they cover the recommended terms. It includes 20 AI Drafts a month on both plans; extra Drafts cost more.

If your bottleneck is getting drafts written, Frase saves more time. If your bottleneck is quality control on human-written content, Clearscope's grade is easier to hand to a team.

## Scoring: three scores vs one grade

Frase splits optimization into **SEO** (vs top results), **GEO** (likelihood of being quoted by AI answers) and **EEAT** (author and experience signals). Our test draft: 94% GEO, 78% SEO, 33% EEAT. The low EEAT score is useful: it tells you exactly what a human must add.

![Frase GEO score breakdown](/img/frase/23_geo_score.jpg)

Clearscope's single grade is simpler and widely praised for being intuitive. Reviewers say it makes optimization feel fast.

## AI search visibility

Both now track AI answers. Frase includes AI Visibility on every plan, with engines growing by tier (Perplexity on Professional; Claude and Gemini on Scale), and shows which sites AI answers cite. Clearscope's 2025 "2.0" relaunch added tracking of brand and query mentions in ChatGPT and Gemini, plus query fan-out insights.

## Cost for a real team

Here's how the two compare for three common setups, using list prices checked November 2026:

| Setup | Frase | Clearscope |
|---|---|---|
| 1 writer, 8 articles/month | Starter: $39/mo yearly | Essentials: $129/mo |

For one person, Frase is clearly cheaper. For mid-sized teams the gap narrows, because Clearscope doesn't charge per user. But note what each price includes: Frase's article credits are full researched drafts, while Clearscope's 20 Drafts are a smaller part of a grading-first product.

## Workflow example: one comparison article

**With Frase:** run research on "X vs Y" (about 5 minutes), pin keywords, competitor headings and citable stats, choose the Comparison Article brief type, generate the brief, then let Frase write. Spend your time on the verdict, your own screenshots and fact-checking. Our [AI writer guide](/frase-ai-writer/) shows each step.

**With Clearscope:** create a report for the keyword, write or paste your draft, and work through the recommended terms until the grade reaches A or above. Use a Draft credit if you want AI help with a section.

Both routes produce an optimized article; Frase does more of the writing, Clearscope gives a cleaner grading experience.

## Pros and cons

### Frase

**Pros:** much cheaper entry; full AI drafts; deep research with citable evidence; AI tracking on all plans; no-card trial.
**Cons:** paid seats; weak EEAT on raw drafts; credits expire monthly; rewrites cost credits.

### Clearscope

**Pros:** clean, intuitive editor; unlimited users; highly rated (4.9/5 G2); 14-day trial.
**Cons:** expensive for solo users; credit limits; fewer drafting features; refunds handled case by case.
