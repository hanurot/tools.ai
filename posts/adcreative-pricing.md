## How credits work

- **Generating is unlimited** on every plan.
- **One credit = one downloaded creative.**
- Credits renew every month and **don't roll over**.

This is good news for testing: you can generate 100 variations and only pay for the 5 you actually use.

## What every plan includes

According to AdCreative.ai's pricing page, all plans include:

- Unlimited generations
- Text Generator AI for headlines and ad copy
- Ad platform integrations (Meta, Google, LinkedIn)
- Unlimited iStock photos
- Creative Insights for connected ad accounts

Professional and Ultimate add more brands and users, and video features: product videos, ad videos and storytelling ads. Ultimate is aimed at agencies with up to 25 brands.

## Free trial and refund policy

- **Free trial:** 7 days. Reviewers report it asks for card details, so set a reminder before it ends.
- **Refunds:** within 7 days on monthly plans and 30 days on yearly plans, **only if you haven't used the platform** (generating or downloading counts as use). Refunds are requested through live chat or contact@adcreative.ai and can take 1–2 weeks to appear.

Billing complaints are the most common theme in AdCreative.ai's low reviews on Capterra and Trustpilot. Cancel inside your account settings and keep the confirmation.
