**Short answer:** GEO, or **Generative Engine Optimization**, is the practice of making your content easy for AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude) to **find, trust and quote**. SEO gets you ranked in the links; GEO gets you cited inside the AI answer. You do it with clear structure, quotable facts with sources, real expertise, and by tracking which prompts mention you.

## Where the term comes from

"GEO: Generative Engine Optimization" was the title of a research paper by Aggarwal and colleagues, first posted on arXiv in November 2023 and accepted at the KDD 2024 conference. The authors built a benchmark of queries and tested how content changes affect visibility in generative engine responses. They reported that certain methods, such as adding citations, quotations and statistics, could boost a source's visibility in AI answers by up to 40% in their tests.

The term spread fast because the audience moved: Alphabet CEO Sundar Pichai said in mid-2026 that Google's AI Overviews reach about 2.5 billion monthly users, as reported in financial coverage at the time. When an AI summary answers the question, being the source it quotes matters.

## GEO vs SEO

| | SEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Rank in search results | Be cited or mentioned in AI answers |
| Where you appear | Blue links | Inside ChatGPT, AI Overviews, Perplexity answers |
| Main signals | Relevance, links, technical health | Quotable facts, structure, authority, being cited by sites AI trusts |
| Measured by | Rankings, clicks | Visibility %, share of voice, citations |
| Relationship | Foundation | Built on top of SEO, not a replacement |

Good SEO is still the base: AI engines often pull from pages that already rank or from sites they consider authoritative.

## A 7-step GEO checklist

1. **Answer the question in the first two sentences.** AI engines lift short, direct answers. Put a "short answer" at the top of every article.
2. **Write quotable statements.** Turn vague paragraphs into standalone facts: "Frase Starter costs $49 a month, or $39 billed yearly."
3. **Attribute every statistic.** Name the source and date. The GEO paper found citations and statistics among the most effective changes.
4. **Use clear structure.** Descriptive H2s, numbered steps, comparison tables and a bottom-line summary.
5. **Define terms.** Bold a one-sentence definition the first time you use a key term.
6. **Show real expertise (EEAT).** Author bio, credentials, original screenshots and first-hand tests. AI drafts are weakest here.
7. **Get mentioned where AI looks.** Track which domains AI answers cite for your topic and earn coverage on those sites.

## A worked example

Say you write about project-management software and want ChatGPT to mention your comparison of two tools. A GEO-minded rewrite would:

- open with a two-sentence verdict naming which tool fits which buyer;
- add a comparison table with price, seats and key limits, each with "prices checked" dates;
- turn a 150-word paragraph about pricing into three short, sourced facts;
- add a bolded one-line definition of any jargon;
- include your own screenshots and a short note on how you tested;
- track three prompts, such as "best project management tool for small teams", and record which sites get cited.

Then you re-check monthly. If the cited sites are review publications, pitching them or earning a mention there may move the needle more than editing your own page again.

## Common GEO mistakes

- **Chasing a score instead of the reader.** A 100% GEO score on thin content won't earn citations.
- **Unsourced statistics.** AI engines and readers both trust attributed numbers more.
- **Ignoring EEAT.** Our AI draft scored 33% on EEAT; adding real experience is the human job.
- **Tracking too few prompts.** Track prompts across awareness, consideration and decision stages.
- **Expecting instant results.** AI answers change; measure trends over weeks.

## FAQ

**What does GEO stand for?**
Generative Engine Optimization: optimizing content to be cited in AI-generated answers.

**Is GEO replacing SEO?**
No. GEO builds on SEO. Pages that are well-structured, authoritative and already visible in search are more likely to be cited.

**How do I know if ChatGPT mentions my site?**
Use an AI visibility tracker (Frase, Surfer, Clearscope, Writesonic and others) or manually test the prompts your customers would ask.

**What's the fastest GEO win?**
Add a direct short answer at the top, attribute your statistics, and add a comparison table and summary.

**Is GEO the same as AEO?**
They overlap. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is an older term for optimizing for direct answers; GEO focuses on generative AI engines.

**Do I need a paid tool for GEO?**
No. You can apply the checklist by hand and test prompts manually. Tools save time by tracking many prompts and engines and scoring drafts automatically.
