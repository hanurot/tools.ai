## What AdCreative.ai is

AdCreative.ai is an AI tool that generates ad creatives: banners, product photos, ad copy and, on higher plans, video ads. You set up a "brand" with your logo, colors and description, connect your ad accounts if you want, and it produces dozens of on-brand variations in the right sizes for each ad network.

It says it has over 4 million users and more than 1 billion creatives generated. In 2025 it was acquired by Appier, an ad-tech company, and it now sells itself as an "AI tool for advertising" rather than only a banner maker.

## How credits work (read this first)

This is the part most people misunderstand:

- **Generating is unlimited.** You can create as many variations as you want on every plan.
- **A credit is spent only when you download** a creative.
- Credits renew monthly and do not roll over.

So 20 credits on Starter means 20 downloaded ads a month, not 20 attempts. That makes it cheap to experiment, but it also means Starter is small if you run several campaigns.

## Key features

### 1. Ad creative generator

The core feature. Pick a size and a goal, add your headline or let the AI write one, and it produces many banner variations using your brand colors, logo and stock or product images. It's built for direct-response ads (sales, sign-ups) rather than brand storytelling.

### 2. Product photoshoots

Upload a plain product photo and it places the product into styled scenes (lifestyle backgrounds, studio setups). This is useful for small e-commerce shops that can't afford a photographer for every product.

### 3. Text Generator AI

Ad headlines, primary text and calls to action, written with common copywriting frameworks. It's included on every plan at no extra cost.

### 4. Creative Scoring and Creative Insights

AdCreative.ai scores each creative for predicted performance before you spend money on it. If you connect your ad accounts, Creative Insights analyzes your past ads and shows things like your average CTR against your category and your best-performing colors.

Treat the score as a guide for which variations to test first, not a guarantee. The only real proof is running the ads.

### 5. Competitor Insights

Shows competitors' best-performing ads for inspiration. Reviewers say this works better for e-commerce and consumer brands than for B2B software.

### 6. Video ads (Professional and up)

The pricing table lists Product Videos, Ad Videos, Storytelling Ads, Blog Videos and Fashion Videoshoots. Third-party reviews report that video requires the Professional plan or higher, so on Starter you are working with images.

### 7. Integrations and stock library

You can connect Meta, Google and LinkedIn ad accounts, and every plan includes unlimited stock photos from iStock (AdCreative.ai values this at $900).

## Pros

- **Fast volume.** Dozens of on-brand variations in minutes, in the right sizes for each network.
- **Cheap to experiment.** You only pay credits for what you download.
- **Copy and stock images included** on every plan.
- **Pre-launch scoring** helps you pick which variations to test first.
- **Multi-brand setup** on Professional and Ultimate suits agencies.

## Cons

- **Billing and refund complaints.** This is the biggest red flag. Capterra rates AdCreative.ai 3.3/5 from about 170 reviews, and many low scores are about being charged at sign-up, auto-renewal and slow refunds. G2 is kinder at around 4.3/5.
- **Strict refund policy.** Refunds are available within 7 days on monthly plans and 30 days on yearly plans, **only if you haven't used the platform.** Generating a single creative counts as use.
- **Templated look.** Output can look generic, and you get less control than in Canva or Figma.
- **Big jump for video.** Video needs Professional at $249/month, a big step from $39.
- **Starter is small.** 20 downloads, 1 brand and 1 user go quickly if you run several campaigns.

## Who should use AdCreative.ai

**Good fit:**

- E-commerce stores running Meta or Google ads that need fresh creatives every week.
- Performance marketers who A/B test many variations.
- Agencies managing several brands (Professional covers 10 brands and 10 users).

**Not a good fit:**

- Brands that need highly custom, artistic design. Use a designer or Canva.
- People who run one or two ads a month. Starter will feel expensive per ad.
- Anyone who needs video ads on a small budget.

## How to test AdCreative.ai safely

Because of the billing complaints, use the trial carefully:

1. **Before you start,** check whether the trial asks for card details. Several reviewers say it does.
2. **Set a calendar reminder** for day 5 or 6 of the 7-day trial.
3. **Day 1:** create one brand with your logo, colors and a clear product description.
4. **Days 2–4:** generate creatives for one real campaign. Generate freely, but download only your best few.
5. **Day 5:** run 2–3 of them against your current ads for a small budget.
6. **Day 6:** decide. If they beat or match your current ads, keep it. If not, **cancel inside your account settings** and keep the confirmation email.

## FAQ

**Does AdCreative.ai have a free plan?**
No. It has a 7-day free trial. Paid plans start at $39/month, or less with quarterly or yearly billing.

**What is a credit on AdCreative.ai?**
One credit is used each time you download a creative. Generating is unlimited and doesn't use credits.

**Can I get a refund?**
Only within 7 days on monthly plans or 30 days on yearly plans, and only if you haven't used the platform. Contact them through live chat or contact@adcreative.ai.

**Does AdCreative.ai make video ads?**
Yes, but video features are on the Professional plan ($249/month) and up.

**Is AdCreative.ai good for B2B?**
It can work, but reviewers find its competitor insights and templates stronger for e-commerce and consumer brands.
