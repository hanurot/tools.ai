## Workflow: optimizer vs drafting engine

**NeuronWriter** shows competitors in the SERP, recommends NLP terms and gives a content score as you write. It has AI templates and, from Gold, a Content Designer. Users like the clear guidance and value; many say AI output needs heavy editing.

**Frase** starts with research (keywords, ranking pages, questions, content gaps and citable statistics), lets you pin what matters, generates a brief, then writes the article.

Our test draft was ready in minutes and scored 94% GEO and 78% SEO. Its 33% EEAT score shows the human work still required: author bio, original screenshots, real experience.

## AI-search (GEO) tracking

Frase includes AI Visibility on every plan: visibility %, share of voice, average position, sentiment and the sites AI answers cite. NeuronWriter offers AI Monitoring covering Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT and Perplexity, plus a small number of monitored questions per plan; its add-on pricing sits behind login, so we couldn't verify the cost.

## What users complain about

**NeuronWriter:** AI drafts need substantial rewriting, AI credits run out, the interface feels dated, and long documents can be slow.

**Frase:** credits expire monthly, "Improve scores" and "Rewrite" cost an article each, Starter has one seat, and keyword research is lighter than a full SEO suite.

## FAQ

**Is NeuronWriter cheaper than Frase?**
Yes. NeuronWriter starts at $23/month; Frase at $39/month billed yearly or $49 monthly.

**Which writes better AI drafts?**
Frase's drafts are built from pinned research and verified facts; NeuronWriter users frequently report heavy editing. Both need a human pass.

**Does NeuronWriter have a refund?**
Its pricing page offers a refund within 30 days on your first plan.

**Can I use both?**
Some writers use Frase for research and drafts and NeuronWriter for extra scoring, but most solo users only need one.

**Which tracks AI search better?**
Frase includes AI Visibility on every plan with share of voice, sentiment and cited sources. NeuronWriter offers an AI Monitoring add-on whose price we couldn't verify without logging in.

**Which is better for agencies?**
On a tight budget with many small sites, NeuronWriter's project limits are generous. If the agency sells finished articles, Frase Professional (40 drafts, 5 domains, 3 seats) usually saves more labour.

**Is NeuronWriter good for beginners?**
Its score is easy to follow, but users mention a learning curve and dated interface. Frase's guided research-to-brief flow is friendlier for beginners.
