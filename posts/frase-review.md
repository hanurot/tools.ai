## What Frase is in 2026

Frase started in 2020 as a content-brief generator. It is now sold as an "agentic" SEO and GEO platform. GEO means Generative Engine Optimization: getting your pages cited by ChatGPT, Google's AI answers, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini, not only ranked on Google.

The center of the product is the **Frase Agent**. You describe what you want in plain language ("write a 1,500-word guide on X for beginners and score it against the top 10 results") and it chains the steps: SERP research, outline, draft, optimization, scoring and CMS publishing.

## Key features

### 1. The Frase Agent (80+ skills)

Frase rebuilt its assistant as an agent with more than 80 skills: briefs, drafting, competitor analysis, content audits and more. The useful part is that it runs the steps in sequence instead of making you click through separate tools.

One detail matters if you worry about AI publishing junk on your site: **every content type starts in review mode.** Nothing goes live until you approve it, and you can turn on auto-publish later for content types you trust.

### 2. SERP research and content briefs

Frase pulls the top-ranking pages for a keyword, extracts their headings, the topics they cover and the questions from "People Also Ask", then builds a brief in about a minute. It also flags topics that several top pages cover but your draft misses. That gap list is what actually moves rankings.

### 3. Content score and GEO score

As you write, the editor scores your draft against what's ranking, the way Surfer and Clearscope do. Frase adds a **GEO score** that estimates how likely AI answer engines are to quote the page.

### 4. AI Visibility tracking

Frase tracks whether AI search engines mention your brand or pages. Coverage grows by plan: Starter tracks ChatGPT and Google AI, Professional adds Perplexity, and Scale covers five engines including Claude and Gemini. It also shows which websites the AI answers cite, which tells you where to get featured. Few content tools offer this.

### 5. Content Guard

Content Guard watches published pages for ranking and traffic decay and drafts fixes. It watches 10 pages on Starter, 75 on Professional and 300 on Scale.

### 6. Brand Voice, integrations and API

You can train Brand Voice on your existing articles so drafts arrive in your house style. Frase publishes to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity, Wix and its own FraseCMS, and it offers an API and an MCP server, so you can drive it from other AI tools.

## Pros

- **All features on every plan.** You don't pay more to unlock the agent or GEO tools.
- **Research to publish in one place.** You don't need a separate writer, optimizer and AI-visibility tracker.
- **AI search tracking** is built in, which most competitors don't offer.
- **Safe defaults:** the agent never publishes without approval unless you allow it.
- **Free trial with no credit card.**

## Cons

- **AI prose still needs editing.** Reviewers find the drafts accurate but flat. Treat Frase as a first-draft and structure tool, not a ghostwriter.
- **Keyword research is shallow.** You'll still want Semrush, Ahrefs or a free tool to find keywords.
- **Starter is tight:** one seat, one site, and article credits that expire monthly. "Improve scores" and "Rewrite" each count as one article too.
- **Seven days is short** to judge an SEO tool, because rankings take weeks.
- **Mixed sentiment off G2.** Frase scores 4.8/5 from about 300 reviews on G2, but its small Trustpilot profile is far lower, mostly over pricing and billing. Some long-time users complained when lifetime-deal features moved into paid plans.

## Who should use Frase

**Good fit:**

- Freelance writers who deliver SEO articles to clients and need briefs fast.
- Niche-site and affiliate bloggers publishing on a schedule.
- Small agencies managing several client sites (Professional covers 5 sites and 3 seats).
- Anyone who now gets traffic from ChatGPT or Google AI answers and wants to track it.

**Not a good fit:**

- Hobby bloggers writing once or twice a month. A cheaper optimizer will do.
- Teams that need rank tracking, backlink data and keyword databases in one suite. That's Semrush or Ahrefs territory.

## FAQ

**Does Frase have a free plan?**
No. It has a 7-day free trial that needs no credit card, then paid plans start at $39/month billed yearly.

**Is Frase better than Surfer SEO?**
Frase is the better pick if you want an agent that runs the whole workflow and tracks AI search visibility. Surfer is the better pick if you mainly want on-page scoring inside Google Docs or WordPress.

**Can Frase write in languages other than English?**
Yes. Frase says it researches and writes natively in other languages rather than translating.

**Will Frase publish AI content to my site automatically?**
Only if you turn that on. Every content type starts in review mode.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Yearly plans run to the end of the paid term.
