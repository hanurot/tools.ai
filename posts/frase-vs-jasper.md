## The core difference: search-first vs brand-first

**Frase starts from the search results.** You type a topic, and it runs keyword, SERP, content-gap and evidence research together (about 4–5 minutes in our test). You see what the top pages cover, their word counts and authority, audience questions from Reddit and People Also Ask, and citable stats with sources. You pin what you want, it generates a brief, then drafts the article section by section. Our ~1,500-word draft took about 3–4 minutes.

![Frase research results showing recommended keywords, ranking pages and content gaps](/img/frase/13_research_results.jpg)

**Jasper starts from your brand.** Its strengths are Brand Voices, Knowledge assets, Audiences and a style guide that ground every output, plus a Canvas workspace and agents for marketing workflows. That is valuable when ten people write emails, ads and product pages that must all sound the same. But Jasper has no built-in SERP analysis or keyword research, and its old native Surfer integration is gone: Surfer's integrations page now says the Jasper integration is no longer available. Jasper's July 2026 update added a Semrush integration that feeds keyword data to its GEO Agent, which is a Business-plan feature.

So the question is simple: **does your content need to rank?** If yes, Frase does the research Jasper leaves to other tools. If most of your output is campaign copy, Jasper fits better.

## Content scoring and SEO optimization

This is where the gap is widest. Frase gives every draft three scores. Our AI draft scored **94% GEO, 78% SEO and 33% EEAT**:

![Frase GEO score panel showing 94% with sub-scores for quotability, structure, definitions, takeaways and citable data](/img/frase/23_geo_score.jpg)

- **SEO** compares word count, headings, keyword use, meta tags and readability against the top results.
- **GEO** estimates how quotable the page is for AI answer engines (quotability, structure, definitions, takeaways, citable data).
- **EEAT** flags missing author info, credentials and first-hand media.

The low EEAT score is honest: AI drafts lack real experience, and Frase tells you so instead of hiding it. You still have to add your own tests, photos and author bio.

Jasper has no native SEO content score. Third-party comparisons note that Jasper users who care about rankings typically pair it with a separate optimizer (Surfer, Frase or Clearscope) and paste drafts across by hand. That means two subscriptions and an extra step per article.

**Edge:** Frase, clearly.

## AI search (GEO) visibility

Both tools now track how AI engines talk about your brand, but at very different price points.

**Frase** includes AI Visibility on every plan. In our trial it showed visibility %, share of voice, average position and sentiment, grouped prompts by buyer stage, and listed which websites AI answers cite. For affiliates that last part is gold: it tells you which editorial sites to pitch. Starter covers ChatGPT and Google AI, Professional adds Perplexity, and Scale adds Claude and Gemini. More detail in our [Frase AI Visibility guide](/frase-ai-visibility/).

**Jasper** has a GEO Hub and a GEO Agent. Per Jasper's July 2026 update, GEO Hub covers ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, shows presence, citation rate, sentiment and share of voice, and the agent can run optimization on a schedule. Pricing trackers place GEO agents on the Business plan, with GEO Hub queries and page runs costing credits; Jasper does not publish a price per credit.

**Edge:** Frase for anyone paying out of pocket. Jasper's GEO tooling looks capable for enterprise teams already on Business.

## FAQ

**Is Frase cheaper than Jasper?**
At entry level, yes. Frase Starter is $39/month billed yearly ($49 monthly) vs $59/seat/month yearly ($69 monthly) for Jasper Pro. For teams, Frase Professional includes 3 seats; Jasper charges per seat and quotes Business plans individually.

**Is Jasper good for SEO?**
Jasper can write long-form posts, but it has no native SERP research, keyword research or SEO content score, and its Surfer integration has ended. Its GEO Hub and GEO Agent target AI search visibility on the Business plan. Most SEO-focused users pair Jasper with a separate optimizer.

**Which is better for AI blog writing?**
For blog posts meant to rank, Frase, because it researches the search results first and scores the draft against them. For brand-heavy marketing copy, Jasper.

**Do both track ChatGPT mentions?**
Yes. Frase includes AI Visibility on every plan (ChatGPT and Google AI on Starter). Jasper's GEO Hub tracks ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, but GEO features are reported to sit on the Business plan and use credits.

**Do they have free trials?**
Both offer 7 days. Frase's trial needs no credit card. Jasper's Pro trial is reported to require a card and bills it when the trial ends, so set a reminder.

**Can I use both?**
Some teams use Jasper for campaign copy and Frase for SEO articles. For a solo blogger or freelancer, paying for both rarely makes sense; pick the one that matches where your traffic and income come from.
