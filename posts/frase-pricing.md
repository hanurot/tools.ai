## Cost per article

Billed yearly, Frase works out to roughly **$3.90 per article on Starter, $2.58 on Professional and $2.39 on Scale**, if you use every credit. Monthly billing makes that $4.90, $3.23 and $2.99.

The catch: **credits don't roll over.** If you buy Starter and write four articles, each one cost about $10–12. Pick the plan for your *real* monthly output, not your ambition.

## Hidden costs to know before you buy

1. **"Improve scores" and "Rewrite" each use one article credit.** On Starter, two rewrites turn 10 articles into 8. We recommend fixing EEAT manually instead (see our [AI writer guide](/frase-ai-writer/)).
2. **Audit pages are scarce on Starter** (50 a month). Audit your money pages and top posts first.
3. **Seats, domains and AI Visibility prompts are concurrent limits.** They don't reset monthly; you'd need a higher plan or extra seats.
4. **Extra seats cost $29/month** on Professional and Scale.
5. **Image credits** are separate: purchased credits never expire.

## Which plan should you choose?

**Solo blogger or affiliate site → Starter.** One site, up to 10 posts a month. Pay monthly for the first month to confirm you'll use the credits, then switch to yearly to save $120 a year.

**Freelance SEO writer → Professional** once you have more than one client site or write more than 10 articles a month. Five domains and three seats let a client or editor log in.

**Small agency → Professional, then Scale.** Scale makes sense when you pass about 60 articles a month or clients ask for Claude/Gemini visibility reports.

**Occasional writer (1–3 posts a month) → probably not Frase.** A cheaper optimizer like NeuronWriter will cost less per article. See our [best AI SEO writing tools](/best-ai-seo-writing-tools/).

## Real-world examples: what you'd pay

**Affiliate blogger, 6 posts a month.** Starter yearly costs $468 a year. You'd use 6 of 10 credits, so roughly $6.50 per article. Still cheaper than an hour of freelance editing, but if you consistently publish fewer than 5 posts, consider whether a cheaper optimizer fits better.

**Freelance writer, 3 clients, 20 articles a month.** Starter's single domain and 10 articles won't cover it. Professional yearly ($1,236 a year) gives 40 articles and 5 domains, about $2.58 per article at full use, or about $5.15 at 20 articles. Charge clients accordingly.

**Small agency, 5 writers, 70 articles a month.** Professional's 3 seats plus 2 extra seats ($58/month) and 40 articles fall short on volume. Scale ($239/month yearly) includes 5 seats and 100 articles, which is simpler.

## How to save money on Frase

- **Start monthly, switch to yearly** once you know your volume. Yearly saves about 20%.
- **Don't spend credits on "Improve scores".** Edit EEAT and structure by hand; it's better and free.
- **Pin research carefully** before generating a brief, so the first draft needs fewer rewrites.
- **Use audit pages on money pages first:** top traffic and top-converting posts.

## FAQ

**Does Frase have a free plan?**
No. It has a 7-day free trial without a credit card.

**How much does Frase cost per month?**
$49 on Starter, $129 on Professional and $299 on Scale when billed monthly; about 20% less billed yearly.

**Is the yearly plan worth it?**
Yes, if you've used Frase for at least a month and know your volume. Starter saves $120 a year and Professional about $312.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime; yearly plans run to the end of the term.

**Do unused articles roll over?**
No. Article credits reset each month.

**Does Frase charge for extra seats?**
Yes, $29/month per extra seat on Professional and Scale. Starter is limited to one seat.

**Is there a discount for agencies?**
Frase's self-serve plans don't list one; Enterprise pricing (SSO, white-label, dedicated manager) is arranged with sales.
