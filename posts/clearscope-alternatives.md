## Why look for a Clearscope alternative?

Clearscope is excellent at what it does: G2 users rate it 4.9/5 for its intuitive editor and real-time grade. The common reasons to look elsewhere, based on public reviews:

- **Price:** Essentials is $129/month and Business $399/month. Annual pricing is sales-only.
- **Credits:** 20 Drafts a month on both plans, and monthly credits don't roll over.
- **Limited AI writing:** reviewers note fewer content-creation features than newer tools.

What Clearscope does better than most: **unlimited users on every plan**, and a simple grade that's easy to hand to a team.

## 2. Surfer SEO — closest editor experience

Surfer's Content Editor gives live term and structure guidance against ranking pages, with Google Docs and WordPress integrations. Discovery is $49/month billed yearly; AI tracking starts on Standard ($99), and Pro ($182) tracks five engines daily including Google AI Mode. Users praise the editor; Trustpilot complaints mention expiring credits and support delays. See [Frase vs Surfer SEO](/frase-vs-surfer-seo/).

## 3. NeuronWriter — cheapest Clearscope alternative

At $23–117/month, NeuronWriter offers NLP term recommendations, SERP analysis, AI templates and, from Gold ($69), unlimited team members plus WordPress and Search Console integrations. It's the closest to Clearscope's unlimited-users model at a low price. Expect a less polished interface and AI drafts that need editing. See [Frase vs NeuronWriter](/frase-vs-neuronwriter/).

## 4. MarketMuse — for content strategy at scale

MarketMuse (part of Siteimprove since late 2024) maps topic authority and content gaps across your whole site, turns briefs into drafts and tracks citations in AI answers. Pricing is sales-led. G2 users rate it 4.6/5, praising gap analysis and noting a learning curve and slow analyses.

## 5. Semrush Content Toolkit — for Semrush users

$60/month (billed annually) gets topic research, SEO briefs, a content optimizer, AI rewriting and WordPress publishing. Great value if your team already uses [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/){: rel="nofollow" } for keywords.

## 6. Scalenut — budget AI writer with GEO tracking

[Scalenut](https://www.scalenut.com/){: rel="nofollow" } tracks prompts in ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews and generates GEO articles. List prices are $59–199/month with frequent discounts. Note: no refunds after the 7-day trial.

## How to migrate without losing momentum

1. **List your top 20 pages** by traffic and conversions; these are the ones to re-optimize first.
2. **Run each through the new tool's editor or audit** and note what it flags that Clearscope didn't.
3. **Rebuild your brief template** in the new tool so writers get the same structure.
4. **Run old and new side by side for a month** if your budget allows, then cancel.
5. **Track results by page,** not by tool score: rankings, clicks and AI-answer mentions.
