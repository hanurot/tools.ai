## The core difference: drafting engine vs optimization editor

**Frase is built around the workflow before and during writing.** You type a topic, it runs keyword, SERP, content-gap and evidence research together (about 4–5 minutes in our test), you pin what you want, and it generates a full brief with an outline, key facts and source links. Then it drafts the article section by section. Our ~1,500-word draft took about 3–4 minutes.

**Surfer is built around the editor.** Its Content Editor gives you live term and structure guidelines against the pages that rank, plus a content score that many SEO writers already know and trust. It has an AI assistant (Surfy), a humanizer, an AI detector and plagiarism checks, but its center of gravity is still "make this draft score higher."

That is the real decision. If you start most articles from a blank page, Frase saves more time. If you start from a writer's draft, Surfer's editor is the more established tool.

## Content scoring: GEO score vs Content Score

Surfer's Content Score is one of the most widely used on-page grades in the industry, and its integration with Google Docs and WordPress means writers can optimize without leaving their editor.

Frase splits the score in three. Our AI draft scored **94% GEO, 78% SEO and 33% EEAT**:

![Frase GEO score panel showing 94% with sub-scores for quotability, structure, definitions, takeaways and citable data](/img/frase/23_geo_score.jpg)

- **GEO** (how likely AI answer engines are to quote the page) breaks into quotability, structure, definitions, takeaways and citable data, with tips for each.
- **SEO** compares word count, headings, keywords, meta tags and readability against the top results.
- **EEAT** flags missing author bio, credentials and first-hand media.

The EEAT score is the honest part: it told us the AI draft was weak on experience and authority, which is true of any AI draft. The fix is human work (your own tests, photos and an author profile), not another rewrite.

**Edge:** Surfer for a single, familiar on-page score inside Google Docs. Frase if you care about being cited in AI answers and want the score to tell you what's missing for that.

## AI search visibility tracking

Both tools now track whether ChatGPT and Google's AI features mention your brand. The difference is in who gets it.

![Frase AI Visibility dashboard with visibility, share of voice, average position and sentiment cards](/img/frase/09_ai_visibility.jpg)

**Frase** includes AI Visibility on every plan. In our trial it showed visibility %, share of voice, average position and sentiment, grouped prompts by buyer stage, and listed which websites AI answers cite. That last part is useful for affiliates and agencies: it shows you exactly which editorial sites to pitch. Starter covers ChatGPT and Google AI; Professional adds Perplexity; Scale adds Claude and Gemini.

**Surfer** offers its AI Tracker on Standard and above (not on Discovery). Standard tracks 25 prompts on ChatGPT weekly; Pro tracks 50 prompts daily across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode; Peace of Mind tracks 100. Surfer also sells AI Search Analytics as a standalone plan ($82/month billed yearly). Surfer's coverage of Google AI Mode is a genuine plus.

**Edge:** Frase on entry price (tracking from $39/month). Surfer on engine breadth at the Pro tier and above.

## Pros and cons

### Frase

**Pros**

- Research, brief, draft, score and AI tracking in one tool.
- Every feature on every plan, including AI Visibility and site audits.
- Research surfaces concrete content gaps and citable stats with sources.
- 7-day trial with no credit card.

**Cons**

### Surfer SEO

**Pros**

- Mature, widely trusted Content Editor and score.
- Strong integrations: Google Docs, WordPress, Contentful, Zapier.
- Broad AI tracking at Pro and above, including Google AI Mode.
- Large review base: about 4.8/5 on G2 and 4.9/5 on Capterra.

**Cons**

- No AI tracking on the entry plan.
- No published month-to-month price; yearly commitment for the advertised rates.
- API access only on the top self-serve tier.
- Trustpilot complaints about expiring credits, price hikes and slow support replies (4.2/5 from about 220 reviews).
- Some reviewers report ranking drops after following its suggestions; you still need editorial judgment.

## FAQ

**Is Frase cheaper than Surfer SEO?**
At entry level, yes: Frase Starter is $39/month billed yearly vs $49 for Surfer Discovery. Frase also offers month-to-month billing ($49). At the top self-serve tier both list $299, with Frase's being the monthly rate and Surfer's the yearly rate.

**Which is better for AI writing?**
Frase. Its whole workflow is built to research and draft complete articles from a brief. Surfer has an AI assistant and humanizer, but its strength is optimizing drafts.

**Which tracks ChatGPT and Google AI mentions?**
Both. Frase includes it on every plan; Surfer includes it from Standard upward and offers a standalone AI Search Analytics plan.

**Does either have a free plan?**
Neither has a permanent free plan. Frase has a 7-day free trial without a credit card. Surfer has no free plan; third-party trackers report a 7-day money-back guarantee on new subscriptions.

**Can I use both?**
Some writers do: Frase for research and briefs, Surfer for final optimization. For most solo users, paying for both is hard to justify. Pick the one that matches where you spend most of your time.
